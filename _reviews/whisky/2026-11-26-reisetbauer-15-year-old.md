**Nose:** A bit acrid, fennel, red and black licorice, chalky candies, cough syrup–soaked grain.  
**Palate:** Very sweet. Fruity candy, syrupy, again a bit acrid. Touch of caramel.  
**Finish:** Herbal transitions to sweet, turns almost synthetic. Alcohol and pepper.
{: .npf}

Oddly candied, yet medicinal, yet herbal. ABV just right to just slightly watered down. It is good, but not great. Mostly interesting. Nothing stood out as a flaw, and I had a good time exploring the various aromas and flavors.
