[Lochranza (Arran) 10 Year (2014), Creetown Distillers](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/274855)  
Bottled for Whisky Broker, 234 bottles  
1st fill bourbon cask  
58.1%

Nose: Lemony. Vanilla. Something kind of sweet and chalky, flinstone vitamin-esque. Faint hint of sour body odor. Herbal.

Palate: Odd. Hot burst. Weirdly sweet and dingy. Dried herbs. More flinstone vitamins. Pineapple syrup. Typical bourbon cask notes. Banana.

Finish: A little maritime transitioning to the finish. Palate just drifts off over time, warm and caramel-y. Hot and peppery. Eventually get a little smoke.

Score: 3/5  
Mental Image: Burst of contradictions.

Pretty odd at first, but it grew on me. I found water to make this more boring, though.
