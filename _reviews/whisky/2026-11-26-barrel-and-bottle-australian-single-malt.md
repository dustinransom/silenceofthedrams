**Nose:** Golden straw, caramel, way overripe stone fruit, musty wine, barnyard, salty toffee.  
**Palate:** Old, musty fruit nectar, slightly astringent in an interesting, quirky way, scone, char, golden raisins.  
**Finish:** Straw, slightly bready, and that sweet fruit nectar. A bit hot.
{: .npf}

Reminds me of one of my favorite Rest & Be Thankful wine cask Laddies. I wish it had more body, and the finish is a bit underwhelming. But it has fantastic elements... I could nose this all evening.
