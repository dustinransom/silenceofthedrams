[Dailuaine 14 Year (2011), Creetown Distillers](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/276949/dailuaine-2011-whb?language=en)  
Creetown Distillers, bottled by Whisky Broker.  
Calvados barrique finish, cask \# 304725, total of 305 bottles. 

Nose: Candied green apples. Sawdust.

Palate: Sweet, malty. Just a touch of the apple. A rather pleasant pepperiness. A touch of almond extract. Mouthfeel builds a bit, but is overall too thin.

Finish: More of the same, nice transition in the pepperiness. Becomes somewhat juicy.

Score: 3/5  
Mental Image: 3

Quite simple, but very sippable. Lacks complexity and oomph, but well-composed. Nothing to really complain about, but also nothing all that interesting. Quality middle of the road for the right price.
