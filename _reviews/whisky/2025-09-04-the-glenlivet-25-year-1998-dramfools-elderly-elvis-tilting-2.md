This is Dramfool's 2nd iteration of the Elderly Elvis Tilting, which you might notice is an anagram of The Glenlivet. Aged for 25 years in a bourbon barrel from 1998 to 2023.
**Whisky:** The Glenlivet 25 Year (1998), Dramfool's Elderly Elvis Tilting \#2  
**Country/Region:** Scotland/Speyside  
**Cask:** Bourbon barrel  
**ABV:** 55.4%

This has a beautiful and interesting nose. Malted barley, sweet and heathery. Strong bourbon cask influence with almond extract. A bit of absinthe and anise along with Flinstone vitamins I took as a child.

Sipping, I found this whisky to be a rather by-the-book bourbon cask expression. Sweet and spicy bourbon and oak. Some crème brûlée with a hint of cinnamon. Less anise now. Pleasing floral oils and a light fruit syrup.

The finish is expected: warming, oaky bourbon. Not as full or lingering as I would like. The caramel and fruit syrup hangs around as everything fades away, along with a bit of char and almond as I exhale.

Enjoyable and interesting on the nose, but a bit standard fare otherwise. Well composed, aged whisky, but nothing particularly interesting for my taste.

**Rating:** 2.5 / 5

**Mental Image:** Absinthe Flinstone vitamin old fashioned.
