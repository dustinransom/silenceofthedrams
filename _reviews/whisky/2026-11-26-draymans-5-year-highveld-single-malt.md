**Nose:** Macvin du Jura, hot honey, almond, lovely touch of malt.  
**Palate:** Thin, almost gushing with a layer of water, almost cooling. Sweet syrup, citrus, baking spices, and a touch of grain.  
**Finish:** Cinnamon then spice, sweet grain and citrus oil.
{: .npf}

What a divine nose. Reminds me so much of a Benedicte & Tissot Macvin du Jura Pinot Noir. The palate is paltry at 43%, but as lovely as it can be. I'd rate this a 4, but I really can't justify it with the thinness of the palate.
